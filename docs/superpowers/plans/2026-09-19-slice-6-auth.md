# Slice 6 - Auth Implementation Plan (lean process, with a reviewer)

> One Sonnet implementer builds the slice; a Sonnet reviewer checks it (security-critical slice, user decision), then the controller checks it visually.

**Goal:** Amazon's "Sign in or create account" flow with real accounts: the identifier step, password step, create account, sign out, and sessions. Signing in or up merges the guest cart and returns the user to where they came from. The header, flyout, side menu, home band and cart use the session.

**Spec:** `docs/spec.md` 5.7, 6.5, 2 (safety notice); `docs/design.md` 4 (users, sessions), 5.2, 6.6. Recon: `docs/recon/5-*.png` (skip the puzzle, email and phone verification screens: out of scope per spec #21).

## Global Constraints

- Match amazon.com's auth pages exactly: the logo on top, a centred bordered box about 350px wide, 8px radius, Amazon's button and input styles, the minimal footer. Check the live `/ap/signin` page (signed out) with the Claude in Chrome tools. Colours only via tokens; no emojis or long dashes.
- The auth layout shows `SafetyNotice` directly under the box, plus `FooterMinimal` (both exist from Slice 1).
- **Security:**
  - Passwords are hashed with bcryptjs (cost 10) and never logged or returned.
  - The session id is 32 random bytes (hex) stored in `sessions` with a 30-day expiry, in the cookie `session` (httpOnly, `secure` in production, `sameSite: "lax"`, path `/`).
  - Expired or unknown sessions count as signed out.
  - `return_to` must start with `/` and not `//` or `/\`, otherwise `/`.
  - Emails are lowercased and trimmed.
  - Error messages never reveal more than Amazon's do.
- One session object: `getCurrentUser(): Promise<SessionUser | null>` (`React.cache`, per request) is the only way UI and actions learn who is signed in. `SessionUser = { id: string; email: string; name: string; firstName: string }`.
- Anything reading cookies renders inside `<Suspense>` and never inside `'use cache'`.
- TDD for `lib/auth/*` and the Zod schemas. Test, lint, typecheck and build must pass. Commit with `.agent-logs/` changes, message ending `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Do not push.

**Contracts (later slices use them):**
- `hashPassword`, `verifyPassword`, `createSession(userId)`, `destroySession()`, `getCurrentUser()`
- `requireUser(returnTo: string): Promise<SessionUser>`, which redirects to `/ap/signin?return_to=<encoded>`
- `safeReturnTo(value): string`
- `findUserByEmail`, `createUser`

**Flow and copy (Amazon's wording; confirm on the live page):**
1. **`/ap/signin?return_to=`:**
   - A box titled "Sign in or create account"; the label "Enter mobile number or email", an input, and a yellow full-width "Continue".
   - Small print: "By continuing, you agree to Amazon's Conditions of Use and Privacy Notice." (links from `FOOTER_LEGAL`), "Need help?" (to `/customer-service`), a divider, "Buying for work?" and "Create a free business account" (external `https://www.amazon.com/business`).
   - Submit action `identify`:
     - Empty input: an inline alert "Enter your mobile number or email".
     - Phone-shaped input (`/^\+?[\d\s()-]{7,}$/`): "We cannot find an account with that mobile number".
     - Otherwise it must be a valid email, or it shows "Invalid email address" (confirm the live wording).
     - A known email redirects to `/ap/signin/password?email=&return_to=`; an unknown one redirects to `/ap/register?email=&return_to=`.
2. **`/ap/signin/password`:**
   - Title "Sign in"; the email shown with a "Change" link (back to step 1 with `return_to` kept); "Password" label with a "Forgot password?" link that opens the external Amazon help page in a new tab; a password input; a yellow "Sign in"; "Keep me signed in" is not needed.
   - A wrong password shows the red-bordered alert box "There was a problem" / "Your password is incorrect".
3. **`/ap/register`:**
   - Title "Create account"; "Email" showing the email and "Change"; "Your name" (placeholder "First and last name"); "Password (at least 6 characters)" with the info hint "Passwords must be at least 6 characters."; "Re-enter password"; a yellow "Continue"; a divider; "Already a customer?" + "Sign in instead"; small print about Conditions of Use and the Privacy Notice.
   - Validation messages under the fields in Amazon's red with the alert icon: "Enter your name", "Minimum 6 characters required", "Passwords must match".
   - A duplicate email shows the alert "There's already an account with this email" with a "Sign in" link.
4. **On success (sign in or register):**
   - `createSession`.
   - `mergeGuestCart(guestToken, user.id)` when a guest cart exists, then clear `cart_token`.
   - Redirect to `safeReturnTo(return_to)`.
   - (Browsing-history merge and the default list are added by their own slices.)
5. **Sign Out** (flyout and side menu) is a form posting to the `signOut` action: `destroySession`, then redirect to `/`.
6. **Already signed in** and visiting `/ap/signin` or `/ap/register`: redirect to `return_to` or `/`.
7. **Header:** "Hello, Ahmar" plus "Account & Lists"; the flyout shows "Your Lists", "Your Account" and "Sign Out" (plus a "Switch Accounts" link to `/ap/signin`), without the Sign in button. The side menu header shows "Hello, Ahmar". The mobile header shows "Ahmar ›" with the person icon, linking to `/your-account`.

**Mobile:** the auth pages are the same centred box at full width minus 16px margins, with the logo on top.
