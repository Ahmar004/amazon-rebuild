# Slice 10 - Lists Implementation Plan (lean process)

> One Sonnet implementer; the controller checks it visually.

**Goal:** Amazon's "Add to List" on the product page (dropdown of the user's lists plus "Create a List") and the Your Lists page (lists sidebar, items, move to cart, remove, rename, delete).

**Spec:** `docs/spec.md` 5.5 (Add to List), 5.10 (Your Lists); `docs/design.md` 5.3 (lists.ts), 6.10. Recon: `docs/recon/2-search-result-product-page-scroll-1.png` (the "Add to List" button under the buy box).

## Global Constraints

- Match amazon.com: check a live product page's "Add to List" control while signed out, and the lists page layout on amazon.com. Colours only via tokens; no emojis or long dashes.
- Signed out: "Add to List" redirects to `/ap/signin?return_to=/dp/<asin>`. `/lists` uses `requireUser`. Every query filters by `userId`; a foreign list id gives not found.
- The default list is named "Shopping List" and is created on first use (`ensureDefaultList`). Also call it after sign-in and register (add the call to Slice 6's success path).
- No duplicate items in a list (primary key `(listId, asin)`; adding again is a no-op with the message "Already in your list").
- TDD for the list data helpers (default creation, duplicate handling, ownership) and the list-name schema (1 to 50 characters). Test, lint, typecheck and build must pass. Commit with `.agent-logs/` changes, message ending `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Do not push.

## Task 1: Lists, end to end

**Files:** `lib/data/lists.ts`, `actions/lists.ts`, `lib/validation/lists.ts`, `components/lists/*` (`AddToList` with its dropdown and the "Create a List" modal, `ListSidebar`, `ListItemRow`), `app/(shop)/lists/page.tsx` (redirects to the default list), `app/(shop)/lists/[listId]/page.tsx`; modify the product page buy box (add `AddToList` under the purchase buttons).

**Look and behaviour:**
- **Buy box:** a full-width white bordered "Add to List" button with a caret section on the right. Clicking the main part adds to the default list and shows a small confirmation popover ("1 item added to Shopping List" with a "View Your List" link). The caret opens a dropdown of the user's lists (click one to add) plus "Create a List", which opens a modal: "Create a new list", a "List name" input defaulting to "Shopping List 2", then "Cancel" and a yellow "Create List" button. Creating a list also adds the product to it.
- **Your Lists page:** the title "Your Lists" with a "Create a List" link.
  - Left sidebar: the user's lists ("Default List" under the default one's name); the selected list is highlighted with a left orange bar.
  - Main area: the list name (24px) with a "More" menu ("Rename list", "Delete list"; delete confirms, and the default list can't be deleted).
  - Items: image, title link, stars + count, price, "Item added <Month D, YYYY>", a yellow "Add to Cart" button (adds with `redirectTo: "none"`) and a "Delete" button.
  - Empty list: "There are no items in this list."
- **Mobile:** the sidebar becomes a list selector at the top, with stacked item rows.
